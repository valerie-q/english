📖 ↓ FOLLOW THE PLOT — READ & DISCUSS
🎣 SCENE 1 — THE TRAP IS SET
"The fish have taken the bait!" — who are "the fish"? What's the "bait"?
Burns says he only needs their luggage. Why? What does he plan to hide in it?
Homer signs up the whole family for a contest he knows nothing about. Very Homer. Would you do the same?
"The last four names on the list are not really employees…" — does Burns care? What does this tell you about him?
Why does Burns want a FAMILY specifically — not just one employee?
✈️ SCENE 2 — ON THE PLANE
"Miss, do you have a vegetarian meal that is low in sodium and dairy-free?" — "You have a choice. Pork au gratin or… go hungry! We're out of chicken."

Discuss:

Lisa asks for a very specific meal. Is this realistic? Have you ever been in a situation like this?
Bart says the guy next to him is "creepy". Homer is literally thinking about how fluffy the clouds look. What does this scene tell us about each character?
Burns is secretly watching the family. Does Homer suspect anything? How do you know?
🌴 SCENE 3 — WELCOME TO WANNASI ISLAND
Homer says to Smithers: "Hey, you look just like my boss!" Smithers says: "I am your boss, you insipid gibbon!"
Burns is trying to keep his cover. Is he good at pretending? Find evidence.
Burns has hidden a map in the Simpsons' bungalow. What do you think happens next?
🏝️ SCENE 4 — "ISLAND LUXURY" (NOT REALLY)
Homer is in a hammock, Bart is watching TV. Marge says they should "do things we can't do at home." Are they? What are they actually doing?
The maid gives them P.O.P.S. — Pieces of Overpriced Plastic — as "gifts from the Welcome Committee." Have you ever been given useless tourist gifts?
Look at what the maid is secretly doing. Who is she working for? What does putting tracking devices on the kids say about Burns?
Bart says: "The bumblebee man waits for nobody!" — what does this tell you about Bart's priorities on a tropical island?

🗺️ SCENE 5 — THE TREASURE MAP (AT THE LUAU)
Homer finds a map but has no idea it's Burns's contraband map. He just thinks it's a treasure map. Is this good or bad for Burns's plan?
"Treasure? Goddess? Pirates?" — three people react differently to the same information. Which reaction is most like you?
A local man asks Homer: "Did you find any unusual objects d'art or rare island tidbits?" Homer says no. Is he lying or just oblivious?
At this point in the story — who is in control: Burns or the Simpsons? Who has the advantage?
💥 SCENE 6 — THE ACCIDENTAL HERO
At the airport Burns gets arrested. Homer burps up the D-T36 he accidentally swallowed. Everyone thinks he outsmarted Burns. Did he? What actually happened?
Wiggum says: "You've outsmarted us all, haven't you?" and Homer says: "Uh… well, you see…" — what is Homer thinking at this moment?
Burns says: "If it weren't me you were trying to extort, I'd admire your cunning." Is Homer cunning? What's the difference between being clever and being lucky?
Homer gets taken to hospital to have his stomach pumped. Bart says: "Cool!" — very Bart. What would you say?

3 They're followed, tracked, and accidentally led to a cave with the contraband — while Burns watches their every move.

5 No — Homer has no idea. He thinks it's a real vacation prize.
