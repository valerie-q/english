⏱️
Revision B — Present Simple or Continuous? 1.5 Grammar 6 min

Choose the correct form and explain why in one word.

  1. She (manage) the finance team — it's her permanent role.
  2. He (act) as Head of Finance while his boss is on leave.
  3. I (think) you'll find the induction very useful. (= opinion)
  4. We (meet) the clients tomorrow at 10am — it's all confirmed.
  5. She (have) breakfast right now. (= eating)
  6. I (know) exactly what you mean. ← can this be continuous?
  7. He (think) about the restructuring plan. (= topic in his mind)
  8. They (deal) with customer complaints every day. (regular)
Answers
  1. manages — permanent situation
  2. is acting — temporary situation
  3. think — opinion (stative)
  4. are meeting — confirmed arrangement
  5. is having — eating (dynamic meaning)
  6. know — NO, stative verb — never continuous
  7. is thinking — topic in mind (dynamic meaning)
  8. deal — regular/repeated action

Grammar · Modals for Instructions
07
Grammar — Modals for Giving Instructions
must · should · ought to · don't · make sure you…
8 min
📐
Grammar reference — modals for instructions & suggestions
ModalUseExample
must / must notstrong obligation / prohibitionYou must leave the building immediately.
should / shouldn'trecommendation / adviceYou should remain calm.
ought torecommendation (more formal)Everyone ought to know the emergency exits.
don't / do notinstruction / prohibitionDo not use the lift during a fire.
make sure (you)instruction with emphasisMake sure you assemble at the designated point.
need tonecessityYou need to check the door handle first.
