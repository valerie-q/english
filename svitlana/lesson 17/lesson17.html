Grammar Reference — p.107

4.2 — Will / Won't: Three Uses
1. Promises and agreements

We use will / won't to make promises and to agree to do (or not do) something.

Thanks for the invitation. I will (I'll) confirm on Friday.

I will not (won't) forget to send the list.

2. Decisions (at the moment of speaking)

We use will / won't to make decisions right now — when you decide as you speak.

A: What time is lunch?   B: I'll check the break times.

My flight is cancelled. I'll stay in a hotel tonight. I won't go home.

3. Offers

We also use will to make offers.

A: I can't use this computer.   B: I'll try and fix it for you.

A: I'm really busy.   B: Don't worry, I'll get something from the canteen for us.

We use an infinitive (base) verb after will / won't.
Subjectwill / won'tInfinitive
+ I / You / We / They / He / She / It will ('ll) phone you tomorrow
check the details
send it immediately
− I / You / We / They / He / She / It won't (= will not) forget to call
charge for Wi-Fi access
miss the deadline

Talking about the future — comparing forms

will / Present Continuous — what's the difference?
FormWhen to useExample
will / won't Decision at the moment of speaking; offer; promise — you decide right now "The train is cancelled. I'll take a taxi."
(decided at this moment)
Present Continuous Fixed, confirmed arrangement — already booked, agreed, or in the diary "I'm flying to Tokyo tomorrow."
(ticket already booked)
2 Choose will or Present Continuous. Write the correct form and explain your choice.
  1. A: We have no coffee!   B: I _____ (go) to get some.  (spontaneous decision)
  2. A: When is your flight?   B: I _____ (fly) to Munich at 8 a.m.  (ticket booked)
  3. A: These bags look heavy!   B: Don't worry, I _____ (help) you.  (offer)
  4. A: When are you seeing the clients?   B: I _____ (meet) them at 2 p.m.  (in the diary)
  5. My train is cancelled. I _____ (take) a taxi to the office.  (decision right now)
Answers
  1. I'll go — will (spontaneous decision)
  2. I'm flying — Present Continuous (already booked)
  3. I'll help — will (offer)
  4. I'm meeting — Present Continuous (arranged, in the diary)
  5. I'll take — will (decision at the moment of speaking)
