6 Write and respond to informal messages with invitations.
A Choose two cards and write an informal invitation message on social media. The teacher writes the other two.
B Respond to each other's invitations, only in social media messages. Say yes to one invitation and no to one.
Useful phrases
Do you want to join me for…? Thanks! I'd love to. Yes! Sounds good. Where? What time? Thanks for the invitation, but I'm not free… Sorry, but I have other plans.

Example messages

A: Hi everyone! We met our sales targets last month! 🎉 Do you want to join me for dinner tonight to celebrate? How about 7 p.m. at the Italian restaurant?

B: Hi team! We're going to move to Lviv soon. Do you want to visit the new office next week? We're going to go on Wednesday morning.

C: Hi all! I got a promotion! 😊 Do you want to go for lunch with me on Friday? I'm going to book a table at 1 p.m.

D: Hi everyone! It's my birthday today! 🎂 Do you want to join me for coffee and cake at 11 in the kitchen?

Replies: Thanks! I'd love to. Where? · Yes! Sounds good. See you there! · Thanks for the invitation, but I'm not free tonight. · Sorry, I'm with some clients at lunchtime. Happy birthday!

7 Write a more formal email with an invitation to a client. BP p.53 · p.118
A very important client is going to visit your company on Thursday next week. You are organising the visit. Write an email invitation to the client.
· Invite the client to have a tour of the company. (Include information on the departments or buildings you'd like to show him/her, and what time.)
· Also invite the client to join you for lunch or dinner on Thursday. (Include information on where, when and who with.)
Use more formal phrases in your email.
Useful phrases
Dear…, We would like to invite you to… Would you like to…? We would like to show you… I look forward to meeting you. Best regards,

Dear Mr Harris,

We are very happy that you are going to visit our company on Thursday next week. We would like to invite you to have a tour of the company at 10 a.m. We would like to show you our new factory, the design department and the warehouse.

Would you like to join us for lunch after the tour? Our Sales Director, Anna Koval, and I would like to invite you to the restaurant at the Grand Hotel at 1 p.m.

I will send you more details soon. I look forward to meeting you on Thursday.
