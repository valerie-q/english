Crazy Invitations 🎉
▾
I invite you to some crazy things. Look who is inviting you — a friend 👫 or a boss / client 💼 — and answer informally or formally.
Say yes to 2–3 invitations. Say no to the others — and think of a funny excuse! 😄
👫 Informal
✅ Thanks! I'd love to. ✅ Yes! Sounds good. ❌ Thanks for the invitation, but… ❌ Sorry, but I have other plans.
💼 Formal
✅ Thank you very much. I would love to. ✅ That would be great. ❌ I'm very sorry, but I cannot come because… ❌ Thank you for the invitation, but I'm not available…

Round 2 — Your turn!

Now you invite me to 3–4 crazy things. Make one formal and one informal invitation.
Do you want to…? · Would you like to…? · We would like to invite you to…
Example answers
  1. Thanks for the invitation, but it's too cold! I don't want to be an ice cream. 🍦
  2. I'm very sorry, but I cannot come because I have a very important meeting… with my sofa.
  3. Thanks! I'd love to. Can I bring a present for your cat?
  4. Thank you for the invitation, but I am not available. I have a presentation about pencils. ✏️
  5. Yes! Sounds good. I'll bring popcorn! 🍿
  6. Thank you very much. That would be great.
  7. Sorry, but I have other plans. I'm going to sleep until 12. 😴
  8. I'm very sorry, but I cannot come because I don't have a warm coat.
