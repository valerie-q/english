Grammar — Talking about the Past
We use the Past Simple to talk about completed actions and events in the past. We often use specific time references (e.g. in 2017, last year) to say when the action happened.

I started the business in 2017.
We launched the new product last year.
Past Simple positive
+I/You/We/They/He/She started the business in 2017.
+I/You/We/They/He/She launched the new product last year.
+I/You/We/They/He/She applied for the job last week.

Spelling of Past Simple regular verbs — add -ed to the end of the verb.

consonant-evowel + -y or -wconsonant + -yvowel + consonant
work → workedproduce → producedstay → stayedapply → appliedstop → stopped
start → startedhire → hiredshow → showedstudy → studiedplan → planned

Past Simple Yes/No questions

У Past Simple Yes/No questions ми використовуємо допоміжне дієслово did. Основне дієслово (наприклад start, win) не змінюється на Past Simple. Відповідаємо також з did/didn't.

Did you start the business in 2017? → Yes, I did.
Did they hire a new Manager? → No, they didn't.

Subject questions

У Past Simple subject questions головне дієслово (для regular і irregular verbs) стоїть у формі Past Simple. Ми не використовуємо did.

Who started the business?
Who won the contract?
Subject questionAnswer
Who started the business?Three friends.
Who designed the product?Our Designer, David.
What happened at work yesterday?We had a meeting about the new design.
Which company launched the iPhone?Apple. Apple launched the iPhone.
Which employee started last week?David Lansbury. David Lansbury started last week.
Ключова різниця: в object/yes-no questions питальне слово замінює об'єкт → потрібен did + дієслово без -ed. В subject questions питальне слово замінює підмет → без did, дієслово одразу в Past Simple.
