Read the five emails between David and Susan. Number them in the correct order (1–5).

✉️  Email A  — Susan to David   Order:

✉️  Email C  — David to Susan   Order: 1

I hope you are well. As you know, we need to have a new project planning meeting by Friday 29 March. I'm available all day on Tuesday or Wednesday. Are you available on those days?

Best regards, David
✉️  Email D  — Susan to David   Order:
Hi Susan,
Perfect. See you at 2.00 p.m. on Thursday, in your office.

Best regards, David
✉️  Email E  — Susan to David   Order:
Hi David,
I have a planning meeting at 1.00 p.m. How about Thursday afternoon at 2.00 p.m.? We can meet in my office.

Best regards, Susan
Correct order
  1. C — David proposes Tuesday or Wednesday
  2. A — Susan: both days busy, suggests Thursday 11:00
  3. E — David: has meeting at 13:00, proposes 14:00
  4. B — Susan: works from home Thursday morning, suggests 13:00
  5. D — David confirms: Thursday 14:00 ✓
