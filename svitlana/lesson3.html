Grammar — Subject and Object questions BP p.15 10 min
Subject vs Object questions
Subject question: Who manages the team? → Pietro manages the team. (підмет = who)
Object question: Which team does Pietro manage? → He manages the sales team. (Pietro = підмет, team = об'єкт)
Пояснення

Subject question — питальне слово замінює підмет. Do/does НЕ потрібне: "Who manages?" — не "Who does manage?"

Object question — питальне слово замінює об'єкт. Do/does ПОТРІБНЕ: "Which team does he manage?"

Make questions — say the answer out loud:

word order help
Who + verb What/Which + does/do + subject + verb
  1. manages / who / the R&D department / ?
  2. does / which department / Svitlana / work / in / ?
  3. works / who / in the Pune office / ?
  4. does / what / Maria / do / ?
  5. manages / who / the wiring project / ?
  6. does / which office / the team / work / in / ?
Answer key
  1. Who manages the R&D department? (subject — no does)
  2. Which department does Svitlana work in? (object — needs does)
  3. Who works in the Pune office? (subject — no does)
  4. What does Maria do? (object — needs does)
  5. Who manages the wiring project? (subject — no does)
  6. Which office does the team work in? (object — needs does)
