Grammar — CAN / CAN'T for possibility BP p.21
CAN / CAN'T
+We can deliver the order on Monday.  ·  I can call you back in 10 minutes.
−They can't deliver today.  ·  I'm sorry, we can't — we don't have any in stock.
?Can you deliver by Monday?  →  Yes, we can. / Sorry, we can't.
Note: can/can't never changes — no -s for he/she. After can, the verb has no -s and no to: She can deliver (not "cans" or "can to deliver").

Roleplay — Phone order ROLEPLAY

Use the example call below as a guide.

📞 EXAMPLE CALL
SUPPLIERGood morning, ConnectTech, Laura speaking. How can I help you?
CUSTOMERI'd like to order some cable connectors, please.
SUPPLIEROf course. Do you have the product reference number?
CUSTOMERYes, it's CC-4471.
SUPPLIERHow many do you need?
CUSTOMERI need 500 units. Can you deliver by Friday?
SUPPLIERI'm very sorry, we can't — we don't have that many in stock. We can deliver 300 by Friday and the rest by Monday.
CUSTOMEROK, that's fine. How much is that in total?
SUPPLIERIt's €180. Shall I confirm your order on the system?
CUSTOMERYes please. Thank you.
  • 1Round 1: You are the customer. Call the supplier and place your order for parts.
  • 2Round 2: You are the supplier (Laura speaking). Answer the phone and take the order. Use: How many? / I'm afraid we can't / I'll confirm your order.
