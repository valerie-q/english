❓
Comprehension check
  1. What did Bill Gross find was the #1 factor for startup success? What percentage did it account for?
  2. Why did Z.com fail, even though it had funding and a business model?
  3. Why was YouTube perfectly timed, while Z.com wasn't?
  4. Gross says Airbnb succeeded partly because of the recession. How exactly did the recession help?
  5. What does Gross say about business model and funding — why did they rank lower than expected?
Key answers
  1. Timing — accounted for 42% of the difference between success and failure.
  2. Z.com launched in 1999–2000 when broadband penetration was too low — people couldn't easily stream video online.
  3. YouTube launched after Adobe Flash solved the codec problem and broadband crossed 50% in the US — the infrastructure was ready.
  4. During the recession people needed extra money, which helped overcome their objection to renting out their home to a stranger.
  5. Business model can be added later if customers want what you're building. Funding is easy to get once you're gaining traction.

05
Grammar — Past Simple vs Present Perfect in real speech
📐
Spot the pattern — from the talk

Look at these sentences from Bill Gross's talk. Each uses either Past Simple or Present Perfect. Identify which tense is used and explain why.

Sentence from the talk Tense Why?
"I've been starting businesses since I was 12 years old."
"In the last 20 years, we started more than 100 companies."
"In 2016, Apple and Google opened their voice API's."
"Up until now, 99% of VUI usage has been about simple questions."
"That company came out right during the height of the recession."
"VUI just hasn't evolved enough for complex tasks yet."
Answer key
  1. Present Perfect — started at 12, still doing it now; "since" signals ongoing period
  2. Past Simple — "in the last 20 years" here frames it as a completed period (Idealab's track record as a finished body of work); note: some speakers might use PP here too
  3. Past Simple — "In 2016" = specific point in time → Past Simple
  4. Present Perfect — "up until now" = from past to present moment → Present Perfect
  5. Past Simple — "came out" = specific past event, Airbnb's launch moment
  6. Present Perfect — "yet" + situation still true now → Present Perfect
