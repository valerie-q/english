Language focus — Verb + Preposition collocations 3 min

The expressions in Exercise 7 are fixed — the preposition is part of the phrase and doesn't change. You need to learn them as chunks, not word by word.

Learn these as fixed chunks
report to sb I report to the CFO. — напрямок підпорядкування
Head of sth I'm the Head of Finance. — посада + відділ
work closely with sb I work closely with the operations team.
look after sth I look after the accounts. — доглядати, відповідати
take care of sth I take care of the budget. — схоже на look after
responsible for sth / doing I'm responsible for managing cash flow.
in charge of sth I'm in charge of the finance team. — сильніше ніж responsible
coordinate with sb I coordinate with all departments.
⚠️
Типові помилки:
✗ I'm responsible of the team. → ✓ responsible for
✗ I report to Sales. → ✓ I'm the Head of Sales.
✗ I'm in charge for it. → ✓ in charge of
