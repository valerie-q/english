Revision B — Future forms Lesson 4 4 min

Choose the correct future form and explain why in one word (intention / arrangement / scheduled / prediction).

  1. The budget meeting at 9am sharp tomorrow.
    a) starts   b) is starting   c) is going to start
  2. I the new analyst next week — we already confirmed it.
    a) meet   b) am meeting   c) am going to meet
  3. She hasn't prepared at all. She the presentation.
    a) fails   b) is failing   c) is going to fail
  4. I a better system for tracking expenses — I decided this morning.
    a) implement   b) am implementing   c) am going to implement
Answers
  1. a) starts — scheduled (timetable)
  2. b) am meeting — arrangement (confirmed with other person)
  3. c) is going to fail — prediction based on evidence
  4. c) am going to implement — personal intention (decided, not yet confirmed with others)

Key ideas from the recording
  1. Silence can feel uncomfortable and is interpreted differently across cultures — some see it as respectful, others as awkward
  2. The content of the question matters less than the act of asking — showing interest in the other person is what builds connection
  3. Asking questions shows you are interested → the other person feels valued → trust begins to build naturally
