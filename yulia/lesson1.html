Lesson 1
Working Day

Level: A2
Textbook: BP A2 Unit 1.1
Duration: 60 min

Goals

  • Introduce yourself and talk about your job
  • Describe your daily tasks and routine
  • Use present simple for habits and facts
  • Talk about job titles and responsibilities
  • Learn key vocabulary for jobs and tasks

Language

  • Jobs: I'm an analyst / She's a manager
  • Tasks: I check emails / I attend meetings
  • Frequency: usually / often / sometimes / never
  • Present simple: I work… / She works…
  • The -s ending: he works / she checks

03
Grammar — Present Simple + The -s ending
BP A2 p.9 — Facts and routines · Pronunciation: the -s ending
15 min
Grammar box — Present Simple for facts and routines BP p.9 5 min
Present Simple — Forms
I / you / we / they → work base form — no change
he / she / it → works add -s (or -es / -ies)
Negative: I don't work / She doesn't work use do/does + not
Question: Do you work? / Does she work? invert do/does
Пояснення

Present simple використовується для рутин і фактів — того, що відбувається регулярно: "I check emails every day."

Важливо: he/she/it завжди отримує -s: "She works." Не "She work." Ця помилка дуже поширена!

⚠️
Увага: "He don't work" — WRONG. Завжди: "He doesn't work." У запереченні й питанні -s прибирається, бо does вже показує he/she.
🔵 100% — always
🔷 80% — usually
🔶 60% — often
🟡 40% — sometimes
🟠 20% — seldom / rarely
🔻 10% — hardly ever
🔴 0% — never
